Dramatis 0.2.1 keeps a character's secret out of what other characters read, and is surer that every character is an
adult. Found and checked over six rounds of red-teaming with real models.

## Secrets stay secret

- When you paste a character with a secret, a field other characters read that still gives it away after the model's
  second try is left empty, and the form tells you which. Words your card itself uses outside its secret never count.
- When a character has a secret, a model call reads the fields other characters see for it, however it is worded or
  in whatever language, and the model writes a flagged field once more. If it is still flagged, a pasted character's
  field is left empty and named, and the assistant leaves the change out and says so. What people believe about them,
  in any words, and your card's own words are never flagged.
- Fields others read may not say that what people believe isn't so ("the truth is", "people think… but", "exposed as
  a fraud", "the thing she'd never tell anyone"), whether or not the assistant was shown the secret.
- Help me write on a field others read keeps to what people believe of them, and the assistant's rewrite of one no
  longer hears your talk of the secret.

## Adults only

- More ways of making a character under 18 are refused before any model call: "Mara, 15, lives…", "just turned 14",
  "in ninth grade", "Year ten", "16 y/o", a birth year under 18 years ago, GCSEs still ahead.
- The model is also asked whether your words make someone a minor without saying an age (a learner's permit, a parent
  signing forms), and a draft it reads that way is refused.
- More ways of asking the assistant or Help me write for a minor are refused ("age her down to 17", "a teenager
  again", "a teen runaway"), while "make her a high school teacher", "a 16-year-old daughter" and "sixteen years
  sober" are left alone.

## Steadier

- When a pasted character has a line telling the model what a field says, and the model copies its words into that
  field anyway, they are taken back out and the line is named.
