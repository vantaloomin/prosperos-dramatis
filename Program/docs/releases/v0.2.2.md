Dramatis 0.2.2 loses far less of a character to the secret check that 0.2.1 added, and makes the Coming-of-age cast
template about adults.

## Secrets, with less lost

- When the check still finds a secret in a field other characters read after the model's second try, only the
  sentence or list item that gives it away is taken out. The field is left empty, and named at the top of the form,
  only when nothing else is in it. In test runs with real models, the fields lost over 40 pasted characters fell from
  61 in 0.2.1 to 34, and the assistant lost its whole change on 1 request in 40, down from 9.
- The assistant keeps the rest of a change too, and says "I took a line out of my change to Background".
- A line that denies what people believe ("she never finished the conservatory") is caught, where it used to pass as
  the cover story's own words.
- The check no longer takes out a field over words that are not in it: the secret quoted at a Background that says
  nothing like it, two list items it joined, or its own paraphrase of a sentence.
- A pasted character's fields no longer hint that there is a secret ("avoids talking about her training", "guilt
  about something") unless your text says so.
- A small model (around 12B) is a poor judge here: it reads ordinary lines as hints and takes out more than it should.
  Give Writing and the Assistant a larger model if your characters keep secrets. To keep a detail in view, such as
  the job they held, write it into what people believe.

## Casts

- The Coming-of-age template is about adults in their twenties finding their place: the hero, the friends, the family
  and the rival circle. Casts already made from it are unchanged.

## In step

- The vendored formats match Companion's main again. Nothing about a character changed there.
