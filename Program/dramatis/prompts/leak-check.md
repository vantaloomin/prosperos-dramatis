A field gives the secret away when someone who read only that field could learn or guess something that only the secret says: it tells what really happened, in the secret's own words or in other words, or it hints that they hide something, that there is more to a story, or that what people believe is wrong. Judge each field by what it means, in whatever language it is written.

A field never gives the secret away by saying what people believe. What people believe is about the same events, places and people as the secret, so a field may name them and tell the believed story about them in any words and with any detail that fits it: if people believe she left a job for nursing, "left the county job for the better hours" keeps the secret. Ordinary things about them that have nothing to do with the secret keep it too, even when they share a word with it. Flag only what the secret alone could tell someone, and when you are not sure, do not flag it.
